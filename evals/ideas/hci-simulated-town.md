I want to build a small interactive town, like a life-simulation game, populated by about two
dozen characters that are each driven by a large language model. Every character keeps a running
natural-language record of what it observes, retrieves memories by how recent, important and
relevant they are, periodically condenses them into higher-level conclusions about itself and
others, and plans its day, revising the plan when something happens. Users can watch the
characters and talk to them. The goal is believable behaviour of individuals and groups, for
example news spreading through the town or characters organising a party together.
